Privacy Policy

Battle Balls · last updated 25 October 2026

Battle Balls is a mobile game made by an independent developer ("we"). This page says what the game collects, why, and how to delete it. We don't show ads, we don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • A player id. When the game first goes online it creates an anonymous guest account. It has a random id and no name, email or device id.
  • Your email, if you give it. Only when you choose to save your progress to an account or sign in. We use it to send you sign-in codes and nothing else.
  • Your game save. Your critters, gear, currencies and progress, so you can carry on from another device.
  • What other players see. Your player name, friend code, trophies, Ghost Arena team and its saved shots, Bounce Run scores and Bracket picks.
  • Play stats. Small anonymous game events such as "fight ended, won, 32 seconds" or "tutorial step 3", used to balance critters and fix the tutorial. They never include names, free text or device ids. You can turn them off in Settings › Play stats, and they're off by default if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We get the item you bought and a transaction id so we can deliver it. We never see your card details.

Where it's kept

Game data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. The web version is hosted by Vercel. Both process data only on our behalf. The game also keeps a copy of your save on your device.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, everything above is deleted from our servers straight away, except the record of purchases that Apple or Google keep for themselves.

Deleting your account

In the game, tap your level badge to open Account, then Delete account. This deletes your account, save, friends, scores and play stats for good, and the device starts again as a new guest. If you can't open the game, ask us on the support page and we'll do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through the support page. If you're in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Battle Balls is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect an email from a child under 13. If you think a child has given us one, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the game if the change matters.
