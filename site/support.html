Support

Battle Balls

Need help? Report a problem, ask a question or request your data at github.com/Newmsy/BattleBalls/issues. Please include the version line from the bottom of Settings. Don't post your email address there: we'll never need it to help you.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my critters?

On the old phone, tap your level badge, then save your progress with your email. On the new phone, tap the level badge, choose Sign in instead and use the same email.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your level badge, then Delete account. It deletes everything for good. See the Privacy Policy for what that covers.

A purchase didn't arrive

Open the Shop, tap your gems, then Restore purchases. If it's still missing, report it with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

How do I turn off play stats?

Settings › Play stats › Off. Events not yet sent are dropped and the ones already sent are deleted.
